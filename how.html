Plain language

Who does what

No dashboards full of jargon. One shop. Agents behind the counter. A paywall where a person is still required.

You open the shop

The landing page and catalog are public. Looking is free.

You pay only when the action needs a person

Member unlocks orders and small refunds. Operator unlocks the approval desk. The owner key is not for sale.

Agents do the rest

Sales, support, pricing, stock, books, and security keep running. They publish status. They do not spend large money without a yes.

The owner can stop the line

The emergency page pauses selling, ads, or refunds. It asks for the key stored in Vercel, not in the page.
